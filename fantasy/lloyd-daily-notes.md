## Trade evaluation — 22 Oct 2026: LaMelo Ball (Top G) for Anthony Davis, 1-for-1

ESPN `appliedAverage` for a **projection** contains base scoring + GP only — no OREB, DD,
TD or TW. Those were measured empirically instead, by taking each player's **2025-26
actual** `appliedAverage` and subtracting the hand-computed base + GP; the remainder is
that player's real earned bonus rate per game.

| | Proj base+GP /g | Measured bonus /g | Full /g | Proj GP | Season |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anthony Davis | 50.56 | **+5.55** | **56.1** | 61 | **3,423** |
| LaMelo Ball | 47.43 | **+1.94** | **49.4** | 68 | **3,357** |

- AD's bonus edge is mostly **OREB (3.1 vs 0.9)**, then the double-double (~68% vs ~20%).
- Per game AD is +6.7.
- ⚠️ **CORRECTED 22 Oct 2026.** My first pass compared raw season totals
  (full/g × projected GP) and called it a 66-point tie. That was wrong: it treats every
  missed game as a **zero**, when a missed game is actually backfilled from the bench at
  ~32/g (Coward, Murray-Boyles). The right currency is **value over replacement**:
  AD **+24.1** per game played, LaMelo **+18**. Break-even is `18 × 68 / 24.1` =
  **~51 games for AD, not 60** — and ~46 if LaMelo misses time too (he played 72 last
  season but 47, 36 and 22 in the three before). **There was never a tie; AD wins
  comfortably.** Never compare season totals in this league without netting out
  replacement level.
- Positions: LaMelo is PG/G/G-F only but would be the **second PG-eligible player**,
  patching the Dejounte single-point-of-failure. AD's PF/C minutes are covered by Wemby,
  Giannis, Edey and Murray-Boyles.
- Lloyd has AD 25th in ESPN points and LaMelo 14th but says LaMelo is **too high — move to
  19-24**: "Points tier 4, 35th in points leagues last season. Not as good in points
  leagues as in category leagues." So he effectively has them adjacent.
- LaMelo is now on **Minnesota**, not Charlotte. ESPN projects him *up* to 32.9 min /
  23.9 pts / 8.3 ast next to Edwards, after 28.0 min / 20.1 / 7.1 last season. No Lloyd
  preview exists for Minnesota or Washington in my notes — that read is missing.
- LaMelo played **72 games last season**, which cuts against his fragile reputation, but at
  a managed 28 minutes.
- Top G holds **eight guards and Jimmy Butler is OUT** — they need a big badly, so this is
  very acquirable. That cuts both ways.
- ⚠️ **I floated Karl-Anthony Towns as a better ask and then withdrew it.** The
  derived gap was KAT 51.1/g vs LaMelo 49.4/g — **1.7/g, which is inside the error of
  this method**, since the bonus rate is last season's applied to a new projection and
  LaMelo changed teams with assists going 7.1 → 8.3 (adjust for that and he is ~50.5, a
  dead heat). I also have **no Lloyd rank for KAT** beyond the +14 format adjustment,
  against an explicit Lloyd read on LaMelo, and KAT produces almost nothing at the two
  +4 categories. Do not present a sub-2-point/g derived gap as an edge.
  (Haliburton has no measured bonus rate — he missed all of last season.)

**Verdict: hold Anthony Davis.** The per-game edge is ~6 and the games-played gap is
mostly absorbed by the bench. The only case for LaMelo is the tail where AD plays under
~50 games — a real risk (20 last season) made worse by a replacement-level bench, but not
the base case. Top G needs a big; do not sell them one at a discount.

### Re-asked 23 Oct 2026 — verdict unchanged, margin wider

The 22 Oct numbers all re-derived identically from the API (and the method self-checked:
*both* players' projection rows returned a measured bonus of exactly **0.00**, confirming
rule 8 — projections carry no OREB/DD/TD/TW).

**What changed is LaMelo's minutes, via the lesson learned on Buzelis the same night.**
ESPN projects him **up** to 32.9 mpg; he played **28.0** last season and is at **26.0**
through two games (29, then 23). Minutes are the largest term in his value, so:

| LaMelo at | full /g | VOR vs 32 | AD break-even |
|---|---|---|---|
| 32.9 mpg (ESPN proj) | 49.4 | 1,183 | **49 games** |
| 28.0 mpg (last season) | ~42.3 | 700 | **29 games** |
| 26.0 mpg (actual so far) | ~39.4 | 503 | **21 games** |

Caveat: scaling base scoring linearly with minutes is crude — per-minute rate is not
constant and assists may not scale with playing time. Directional, not precise.

**AD's minutes are tracking fine** — 30.0 mpg over two games vs 32.2 projected and 31.2
last season. That asymmetry is the argument: AD's projection is credible, LaMelo's is not.

- **PG insurance, priced.** LaMelo is PG/G eligible and Dejounte is the only PG, so a
  Dejounte absence scores **zero** in that slot. Worth roughly
  `games Dejounte misses × 32` ≈ **320 points at 10 games.** That nearly cancels AD's
  287-point edge *if LaMelo hits 32.9 mpg*; it does not touch the ~770-point gap at
  28 mpg. This is the only serious argument for the deal.
- **The AD tail is still real.** At 20 games (last season) his VOR is ~482 and LaMelo wins
  in every minutes scenario. Expected value says AD; variance says LaMelo.
- **Evidence gap, restated:** still no Lloyd preview for Minnesota or Washington — two of
  the eight teams missing from the notes, and the only two that matter for this trade. His
  sole AD remark is incidental, inside the Alex Sarr discussion.
- Colour only, two games: both sat at exactly **37.0/g**. On 23 Oct AD went 26 min / 9 pts
  / 3-11 / 0 ast / 4 TOV while LaMelo went 23 min / 12 pts / 5 ast / 2 stl. AD opened with
  a 68 and followed it with this — high variance, not a trend. Do not act on it.

## Trade evaluation — 22 Oct 2026: Paul George for Kyrie Irving (Top G), 1-for-1

My read disagrees with my own first instinct and with the user's. **Both ESPN and Lloyd
have Kyrie clearly ahead of Paul George.**

| | Proj base+GP /g | Bonus /g | Full /g | Proj GP | **Value over replacement** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paul George | 39.47 | +1.30 measured | **40.8** | 55 | `(40.8−32)×55` = **484** |
| Kyrie Irving | 45.20 | +1.15 **estimated** | **46.4** | 59 | `(46.4−32)×59` = **850** |

- ⚠️ **Kyrie has no measured bonus rate** — he missed all of 2025-26 with the ACL, so
  there is no actual season to back it out of. His +1.15 is an estimate from his projected
  profile (low OREB, almost no double-doubles at 4.8 reb / 4.8 ast, a bad Dallas for TW).
  Least trustworthy number on this page. Same caveat as Haliburton.
- **Lloyd ranks Kyrie 32 and Paul George 56.** Crucially, Lloyd **cancelled his own
  "drop Kyrie to 42-48" call**: *"Is it a little bit too aggressive to have him outside
  the top 40? Maybe he should just be a start of the third round player."* Kyrie's variance
  band is **best 6 / worst 31** — a tighter floor than George's board rank.
- **Lloyd's own sell criterion for George is met.** After the 73-point game he said he
  would move George for *"a top 20 or a top 30 player, top 40 player probably, but that's
  it."* Kyrie at 32 is inside that window.
- Positions: Kyrie is **PG/G/G-F**, so he becomes the **second PG-eligible player** and
  patches the Dejounte single-point-of-failure. Losing George costs an SF/PF where the
  roster is eight deep. Both effects favour the trade.
- "Less upside" does not hold up: George's 73 had **expected FG% under 40%** and
  **2.5 expected steals against 5 actual** — Lloyd put his expected scoring at ~27, not 38.

### The ramp is the real objection, and it is strong

Kyrie has played **one game this season: 24 minutes**, 15 pts, 4 TOV, **25.0 fantasy
points**. The projection assumes **34.6 minutes**. Paul George is already at 31 minutes,
essentially his full projected load.

- Hold Kyrie at ~24 min for 25 games and he produces ~`46.4 × 24/34.6` ≈ **32.2/g, which
  is replacement level** — zero value over replacement for a third of the season. Then 34
  games at full: `14.4 × 34` = 490. Total ≈ **495 vs George's 484. A dead heat.**
- If the slow ramp also costs him games (50 rather than 59): ≈ **365 — George wins by 119.**
- Caveat: scaling production linearly with minutes is rough in both directions — per-minute
  output usually rises as the role opens, but his −18.8/g of FGA scales up too. And this is
  **one game**, not a minutes policy.

**Verdict: do it, and the ramp is what to monitor rather than a reason to pass.** A single
objection that needs to hold for 25+ games to erase a 366-point edge is a monitoring item.
Re-check in two weeks: if Kyrie is still at 24-26 minutes in mid-November, the edge is gone
and holding George was right.
